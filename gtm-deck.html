Where the gap is

You have the facility, the coaches, and the data. What is missing is the path.

  • A parent lands on the site and has one door: join the waitlist. No booking, no response time, no next step.
  • Paid traffic, if it ran today, would arrive with nowhere to land that speaks to softball or to a specific age group.
  • Texts through PlateOps start a conversation, but a conversation is not an appointment on a calendar.
  • Nothing currently ties a signup back to the campaign, ZIP or keyword that produced it.
What this costs

Interest leaks at three points: the first click, the first reply, and the first follow-up. Every leak is a family who chose a competitor with an easier next step.

The fix is not more traffic. It is a shorter, instrumented path from ad to booked assessment, then follow-up that runs without anyone remembering to send it.

Creative

Four angles, refreshed weekly.

Angles
  • Proof. Full Swing KIT numbers on screen: bat speed before and after.
  • Coach credibility. Faces and playing history, short and unpolished.
  • Softball. Speaks directly to families who assume this place is baseball only.
  • Family logistics. One roof, one schedule, one bill.
Formats and cadence
  • Vertical video, 15 to 30 seconds, captioned, shot on a phone in the cages.
  • Three new pieces a week, two tested, one held back.
  • Every ad points at a page that matches its promise.
  • Nothing runs longer than two weeks without a decision.
