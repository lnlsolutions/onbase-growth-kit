Marketing Implementation Agreement
Ad campaign implementation and KPI dashboard · ONBASE Athlete

This Agreement is made on November 19, 2026 between LNL Solutions LLC, an Arizona limited liability company ("Provider"), and ONBASE Athlete ("Client"), with its principal place of business in Mesa, Arizona. Provider and Client agree as follows.

1. Scope of services

Provider will implement the paid acquisition and measurement system described below, for ONBASE Athlete's baseball and softball programs.

  1. Ad campaign implementation. Build and launch campaigns on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google (Search and Local Services Ads where eligibility is approved), including account and pixel configuration, conversion tracking, audience structure, ad copy and creative assembly from Client-supplied assets, landing page guidance, and a naming and UTM convention.
  2. Backend marketing and KPI dashboard. A single dashboard covering waitlist growth, lead source, cost per signup, booked assessments, show rate, membership conversions, and spend pacing, connected to Client's site, forms, booking calendar and ad platforms.
  3. Tracking and attribution setup. Conversion events for waitlist submission, text or chat start, assessment booking, assessment attended, and membership signup, wired to Google Analytics 4, the Meta pixel and conversions API, and Google Ads conversions.
  4. Launch support. Two rounds of revisions on campaign structure and dashboard layout, plus a walkthrough session recorded for Client's team.

2. What is not included

Media spend, third-party software subscriptions, photography or video production beyond editing Client-supplied footage, printed materials, and website development beyond the tracking and landing page guidance described above. Additional work is quoted and approved in writing before it begins.

3. Fees and payment schedule

Implementation fee$1,500 total, payable in two installments.
Installment 1$750 due on signing, before work begins.
Installment 2$750 due December 19, 2026, thirty days after signing.

4. Advertising spend

Media spend is separate from Provider's fees and is funded by Client directly on Client's own advertising accounts, at a budget Client selects and may change at any time. Provider does not bill media, does not mark up media, and does not hold Client funds for advertising. Campaigns pause if the funding account is not current. Client owns the ad accounts, and Provider works within them as an authorized user.

5. Client responsibilities

  1. Provide timely access to the website platform, analytics, ad accounts, booking system, domain DNS and any chat or texting tool used for lead response.
  2. Supply brand assets, photography, program details, pricing and schedule information needed for campaign and landing page work.
  3. Respond to approvals within three business days. Timelines shift day for day when approvals are delayed.
  4. Ensure any claims made in advertising are accurate and substantiated, and that Client holds the rights to any image, video or testimonial supplied.

6. Ownership

Client owns its advertising accounts, website, lead data, customer records and dashboard access. Provider retains ownership of its templates, scripts, internal methodology, reporting frameworks and know-how, and grants Client a non-exclusive license to use the dashboard and reporting outputs delivered under this Agreement for its own business. On full payment, Client keeps the configured dashboard and campaign structures.

7. No guarantee of results

Provider will perform with reasonable skill and care consistent with industry practice. Marketing outcomes depend on market conditions, offer strength, pricing, seasonality, response times and other factors outside Provider's control. Provider does not guarantee a specific number of leads, signups, bookings, members or revenue, and no projection shared before signing is a promise of performance.

8. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential and uses it only to perform this Agreement. Provider may reference Client's name and engagement type in its portfolio unless Client objects in writing.

9. Term, pausing and termination

This Agreement begins on signing and covers the implementation period through January 18, 2027. Either party may terminate with fourteen days' written notice. Fees for work already performed are earned and non-refundable. Continued management, optimization and reporting after the implementation period is agreed separately in writing.

10. Limitation of liability

Provider's total liability is limited to fees paid under this Agreement. Neither party is liable for indirect or consequential damages, including lost profits.

11. General

Provider is an independent contractor; nothing here creates employment, partnership or joint venture. This Agreement is governed by Arizona law, venue in Maricopa County, and the parties will attempt good-faith resolution before other remedies. It is the entire agreement on its subject, replaces prior discussions, and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties. Electronic signatures are valid.
