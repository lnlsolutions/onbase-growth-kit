Tracking feed

Every row is a device event the container receives: a fence entry, an impression, a click, a visit, then a signup. Each number on this tab is the sum of these rows, and a tracked signup writes back into GTM as a conversion.

Fence to signup

A device seen inside a fence is matched to an ad impression, then to a site visit, then to a signup. That chain is what makes a fence measurable instead of just a radius on a map.

What the fences are for

Own facility fences retarget people who already walked in. Competitor fences reach parents standing in another cage. School and sports complex fences reach families before they pick a program. Event fences run for days, not months.
